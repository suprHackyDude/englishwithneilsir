The Niel Method

How he teaches — so you actually start speaking.

  1. Diagnose your voice A short speaking test. We find where you freeze: words, sounds, or confidence.
  2. Speak first, rules later 70% of class is you talking. Grammar is taught inside sentences, not as homework theory.
  3. Correct in the moment Wrong sentence? Fix it live. Repeat it right. The mouth learns faster than the notebook.
  4. Role-play real life Interview, office, college, travel, family — you practise the English you will actually use.
  5. Record, review, repeat Weekly voice notes and feedback so you hear your own progress, not only feel it.
